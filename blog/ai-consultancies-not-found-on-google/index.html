Why Most AI Consultancies Can't Be Found on Google

April 12, 2026 · 5 min read · By Particulate LLC

Search for "AI consulting" on Google. You'll find blog posts from McKinsey, Deloitte, and Accenture. You'll find a few VC-backed startups with $5M in funding. What you won't find are the hundreds of small AI consultancies — the ones actually building agents, automating workflows, and deploying real systems for real businesses.

They're invisible. And it's costing them.

The Problem: AI Companies Build Products, Not Websites

Most AI consultancies suffer from the same SEO failures:

  • No blog content. Zero posts explaining what they do in terms their clients actually search for. Google has nothing to index.
  • Generic messaging. "We leverage AI to transform your business" ranks for nothing. Nobody searches that phrase.
  • No schema markup. Google doesn't know they're a professional service, where they're located, or what they charge.
  • No backlinks. They haven't submitted to any directories, haven't been mentioned anywhere, haven't built a single link.
  • No Google Business Profile. The single biggest local SEO signal — and they don't have one.

The result: when a business owner searches "AI agent setup for my small business" or "hire someone to build an AI agent," they find nothing from the actual consultancies that do this work. They find blog posts and YouTube videos instead.

The opportunity: The SEO bar for "AI consulting" and related terms is incredibly low right now. Most competitors have domain authority of 1-5. A few months of consistent effort can put you on page one.

What AI Consultancies Should Do Instead

1. Write for search intent, not for your peers

Your clients don't search for "LLM fine-tuning strategies" or "RAG pipeline architecture." They search for practical problems:

  • "How to automate customer support with AI"
  • "AI agent for small business bookkeeping"
  • "Hire someone to set up AI agents"

Write blog posts that answer these questions. 400-600 words, optimized for one primary keyword per post. Do this consistently — 2-4 posts per month.

2. Add schema markup to every page

Schema.org JSON-LD tells Google exactly what your business is, where it operates, and what services you provide. It takes 15 minutes to add ProfessionalService schema to your homepage and service pages. Most AI consultancies haven't done it.

3. Claim your Google Business Profile

This is free, takes 20 minutes, and is the single highest-impact local SEO action you can take. Yet most AI consultancies skip it because they think they're "remote-first" or "not a local business." Wrong. Google uses GBP data to understand your service area and business category. Claim it, verify it, and optimize it.

4. Build 10 backlinks fast

You don't need hundreds. For most AI consulting keywords, 10-20 quality backlinks can get you to page one. Start with:

  • AI directories (welcome.ai, topaidirectories.com)
  • Business directories (Google Business, Bing Places, BBB)
  • Industry-specific directories for your niche
  • Guest posts on AI and tech blogs

5. Create service-specific landing pages

One "Services" page with a list of everything you do doesn't rank. Create a dedicated page for each service with 500+ words, unique title tags, and schema markup. Internal link between them.

The Math That Should Convince You

Let's say you're an AI consultancy charging $2,000 per engagement. You write 12 blog posts over 3 months targeting long-tail keywords with 500 monthly searches each. Even with a conservative 2% click-through rate and 5% conversion rate:

  • 12 posts × 500 searches × 2% CTR = 120 visitors/month
  • 120 visitors × 5% conversion = 6 inquiries/month
  • 6 inquiries × 33% close rate = 2 new clients/month
  • 2 clients × $2,000 = $4,000/month in new revenue

That's $48K/year from 12 blog posts and basic SEO. Most AI consultancies are leaving this on the table because they're too busy building cool demos that nobody can find.

Start Where You Are

You don't need a massive budget or a dedicated SEO team. You need:

  1. A blog with 3-4 posts answering real client questions
  2. Schema markup on your service pages (30 minutes)
  3. A claimed Google Business Profile (20 minutes)
  4. 10 directory submissions (2 hours)
  5. Patience — SEO takes 3-6 months to compound

The AI consultancy space is growing fast. The ones who invest in SEO now will own the search results for years. The ones who don't will keep relying on referrals and LinkedIn posts — and wondering why growth feels slow.
