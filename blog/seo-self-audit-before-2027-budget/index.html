How to Audit Your Own SEO Before 2027 Budget Planning

October 4, 2026 · 7 min read · By Particulate LLC

Every October, business owners sit down to plan next year's budget. And every October, most of them guess about marketing: "SEO feels like it's working… let's keep the same line item."

That guess is expensive. Organic search compounds — the gap between a site that's 80% healthy and one that's 95% healthy widens every month. The fix isn't a bigger budget; it's knowing exactly where you stand before you commit money to it.

Here's the self-audit we run clients through, condensed into a 30-minute checklist you can do yourself — no tools to buy, no dashboard to sign up for.

The Five Buckets of SEO Health

Every SEO problem your site can have falls into one of five buckets. When we score a site, each bucket gets a weight based on how much it actually moves rankings for small and mid-size businesses:

BucketWeightWhat it covers
Technical SEO25 ptsCrawlability, speed, mobile, indexing, HTTPS, sitemaps
On-page & content25 ptsTitle tags, headings, intent match, thin/duplicate content
Local SEO20 ptsGoogle Business Profile, NAP consistency, reviews, local pages
Off-site & authority15 ptsBacklink quality, citations, unlinked mentions
Conversion & tracking15 ptsAnalytics, call tracking, forms, page speed for humans

Score yourself honestly on each item below — Yes (full credit), Partial (half), or No (zero). Then add it up.

Bucket 1: Technical SEO (25 points)

  1. Your site resolves to a single canonical domain — https://yourdomain.com, no www duplicates, no HTTP versions live.
  2. Valid SSL certificate, no mixed-content warnings.
  3. XML sitemap exists, is current, and is submitted in Google Search Console.
  4. Robots.txt doesn't accidentally block CSS, JS, or important pages.
  5. No unintended pages are indexed — search site:yourdomain.com and check for staging URLs, tag pages, or thin archive pages.
  6. Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 seconds on mobile (check PageSpeed Insights — it's free).
  7. Site works fully on a phone: tap targets, no horizontal scroll, readable font sizes.
  8. No orphan pages — every page is reachable by crawl from the homepage within 3 clicks.

Bucket 2: On-Page & Content (25 points)

  1. Every money page has a unique, keyword-relevant title tag (roughly 55–60 characters).
  2. Exactly one H1 per page, and it matches what a searcher actually typed.
  3. Meta descriptions exist and read like ad copy — they don't affect rankings, but they affect clicks.
  4. Your top 5 money pages answer search intent: someone landing there cold understands what you do, for whom, in what area, within 5 seconds.
  5. No thin pages — anything under ~300 words of real content either gets built out or consolidated.
  6. No duplicate content across pages with slightly different URLs.
  7. You have at least a handful of pages targeting informational queries ("how to", "what is", "cost of") — not just service pages.
  8. Internal links use descriptive anchor text, not "click here."

Bucket 3: Local SEO (20 points)

  1. Google Business Profile claimed, fully filled out, with current hours, photos, and services.
  2. Business name, address, and phone are character-identical across your website, GBP, and every directory.
  3. You're listed on the major citations: Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp (if relevant), and your industry's main directories.
  4. You have a review generation process — not a one-time blast, but a steady stream.
  5. You respond to every review, good or bad, within a few days.
  6. Service-area pages exist for each town/region you actually serve (with real local content, not copy-paste templates).

Bucket 4: Off-Site & Authority (15 points)

  1. You know roughly how many referring domains you have (Ahrefs free webmaster tools shows you).
  2. Your links come from relevant sites — local news, chambers of commerce, industry directories — not spam farms.
  3. No toxic link patterns you're unaware of; if you've ever bought links, you know what's out there.
  4. You've claimed unlinked brand mentions (search your company name, ask sites to link it).
  5. Local sponsorships, memberships, or partnerships exist and link to you.

Bucket 5: Conversion & Tracking (15 points)

  1. Analytics is installed and you can see which pages produce leads — not just traffic.
  2. Goal/event tracking works for your main conversion (form fill, call, booking).
  3. Every money page has one clear next action — a button, a form, a phone number — visible without scrolling.
  4. Phone number is clickable (tel: link) on mobile.
  5. Forms are short. Every extra field cuts completion by roughly 5–10%.

Scoring Yourself

Add it up: 31 items, 100 points total. Then read it like this:

ScoreWhat it means
75+You're in good shape — the fixes left are refinements, not fires. A quick audit will find them.
45–74You have real structural problems silently capping your traffic. A deeper audit is worth it before you spend more on content or ads.
Below 45Your site is fighting itself. Fix the foundation first — anything you spend on top of it is leaking.

What This Has to Do With Your 2027 Budget

Here's the pattern we see: businesses that score 75+ usually just need consistent content and citation maintenance — a few hours a month. Businesses under 50 are typically spending money on ads or content that can't convert, because technical or structural problems eat it first.

Run the audit before you write the budget line. If you're healthy, you can spend confidently on growth. If you're not, you know exactly what the first dollar of 2027 should buy: the fixes, not the campaigns.
