Common Questions

Is this a full SEO agency service?

No. We provide AI-assisted analysis, content recommendations, and strategic roadmaps. We tell you exactly what to fix, what to build, and what to prioritize — but you (or your developer) implement the changes. This keeps costs low and you in control.

How fast will I see results?

SEO is a 3-12 month game. Technical fixes can show results in weeks. Content and authority building takes 3-6 months for meaningful traffic gains. We set realistic expectations and track progress monthly.

Do you build backlinks?

We identify backlink opportunities and provide outreach templates, but we don't buy or build links for you. Bought links violate Google's guidelines and risk penalties. We focus on legitimate authority building.

What's included in the audit?

Technical SEO crawl, on-page analysis, keyword gap assessment, competitor comparison, content recommendations, and a prioritized 90-day action plan. Delivered as a written report within 48 hours of intake.
