Common Questions

Is this credit repair?

No. Credit repair companies charge monthly fees to dispute items on your credit report — often things you can do yourself for free. We teach you how to do it and give you the templates, but we don't dispute on your behalf.

Can you negotiate with my creditors for me?

We provide scripts, strategies, and coaching — but you make the calls. This keeps you in control and avoids the fees and risks of third-party debt settlement companies.

What information do you need from me?

For the Snapshot: your total debt, approximate interest rates, and monthly income. For the Payoff Plan: a list of all debts with balances, rates, minimums, and your credit score if you have it. The more detail you provide, the better the plan.

How is this different from free budgeting apps?

Budgeting apps track spending. We help you make strategic decisions: which debts to prioritize, when to settle vs pay in full, how to time disputes, and whether consolidation makes sense for your specific situation.
