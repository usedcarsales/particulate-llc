Common Questions

Is this credit repair?

No. Credit repair companies charge monthly fees to dispute items on your credit report — often things you can dispute yourself for free. We help you understand your full financial picture, prioritize which debts to tackle first, and build a realistic payoff strategy.

Can you negotiate with my creditors for me?

We provide scripts, strategies, and coaching — but you make the calls. This keeps you in control and avoids the fees that debt settlement companies charge (typically 15-25% of your enrolled debt).

What information do you need from me?

For the Snapshot: your total debt, approximate interest rates, and monthly income. For the Payoff Plan: we'll also ask about your expenses, financial goals, and timeline. The more detail you provide, the more tailored your plan.

How is this different from free budgeting apps?

Budgeting apps track spending. We help you make strategic decisions: which debts to prioritize, when to negotiate, whether consolidation makes sense for your situation, and how to protect your credit score while paying things off.
