The 8 Phases of Estate Settlement

From the first phone call through final distribution — here's what every family faces.

1

Immediate (First 24-72 Hours)

Legal pronouncement of death, funeral home contact, death certificates, securing property, locating will/trust documents.

2

Funeral & Memorial (Days 3-10)

Burial vs. cremation decisions, ceremony planning, casket/urn selection, obituary, cost negotiations. Average burial: $10K-$12K. Direct cremation: as low as $995.

3

Government Notifications (Days 7-30)

Social Security, Medicare, DMV, VA benefits, employer notification. Strict deadlines — missing them costs money permanently.

4

Probate & Legal (Days 14-60, 6-18 months)

File the will, petition for executor appointment, Letters Testamentary, estate EIN, creditor notice, asset inventory, court filings.

5

Asset Discovery (Months 1-6)

Locate all financial accounts, insurance policies, unclaimed property, pensions, digital assets. Billions go unclaimed every year.

6

Debt Resolution (Months 2-8)

Identify debts, notify creditors, negotiate settlements, understand what heirs are NOT responsible for. FDCPA protections apply.

7

Tax Filings (Months 3-12)

Final individual return (Form 1040), estate income tax (Form 1041), estate tax (Form 706 if over $13.99M). Step-up in basis can save thousands.

8

Distribution & Closure (Months 6-18+)

Final accounting, court approval, asset distribution, property transfers, estate closure. Executor liability ends with court discharge.
