Common Questions

Is this medical advice?

No. This is a navigation and information service. We help you understand the healthcare system, find providers, decode bills, and prepare for appointments. We never diagnose, prescribe, or replace your doctor's guidance.

What insurance plans do you support?

We can navigate most major US health insurance plans including employer-sponsored, ACA marketplace, Medicare, and Medicaid. Bring your plan details and we'll decode them for you.

How does bill review work?

Send us your medical bill and we'll review it for common errors: duplicate charges, upcoding (charging for a more expensive procedure than you received), unbundling, and balance billing. If we find errors, we provide a dispute letter template and next steps.

Can you help during open enrollment?

Yes! Our Health Advocate tier includes annual open enrollment guidance. We compare plans available to you, estimate total costs, and recommend the best option for your health needs and budget.

How quickly will I hear back?

Quick Check: within 24 hours. Care Path: within 24 hours, with 30-day follow-up. Health Advocate: same business day, with 90-day unlimited support.
