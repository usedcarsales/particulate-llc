FAQ

What AI models do you use?

We use a mix of cloud and local AI models depending on the task. For customer-facing agents, we use enterprise-grade models with guardrails to prevent hallucinations.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We handle all the technical setup. You just tell us what you want automated and we build it.

How long does setup take?

Starter agents deploy in 1-2 business days. Professional and Enterprise setups take 1-2 weeks depending on complexity.

Can agents work with my existing tools?

Yes. We integrate with CRMs, email, calendars, Slack, Discord, phone systems, and most web APIs.
