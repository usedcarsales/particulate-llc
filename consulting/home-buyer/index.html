Things Most First-Time Buyers Don't Know

Buyers don't pay agent commission

Seller pays both agents. You get professional representation for free — always use a buyer's agent.

Pre-approval ≠ final approval

Pre-approval is just an estimate. Final approval comes after underwriting. Don't make big purchases between the two.

Closing costs surprise everyone

2–5% on top of your down payment. On a $350K home, that's $7K–$17.5K you might not be expecting.

Taxes + insurance swing payments HARD

NJ property tax averages 2.4%. A $350K home could mean $700+/month just in taxes and insurance on top of your mortgage.

You can negotiate after inspection

Found issues? You can renegotiate price, ask for repairs, or walk away. The inspection is YOUR safety net.

You don't need 20% down

Conventional: 3% down. FHA: 3.5%. VA & USDA: 0%. The 20% myth keeps people renting for years longer than needed.

First-time buyer programs exist

NJHMFA offers $10K–$15K down payment assistance, special rates (5.375%), and $22K for first-generation buyers.
