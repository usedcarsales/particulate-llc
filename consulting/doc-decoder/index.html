Frequently Asked Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. DocDecoder is an information and navigation service. We explain what documents say in plain English and provide general guidance. For specific legal matters, consult an attorney.

How do you find errors?

We cross-reference your document against known error patterns from industry research. For medical bills, this includes upcoding, duplicate charges, unbundling, and balance billing. For collections, we check FDCPA compliance and statute of limitations.

What if you find an error?

You'll receive a detailed error report and a dispute letter template tailored to your specific situation. Higher tiers include follow-up support to guide you through the resolution process.

How accurate is the analysis?

Our AI catches the most common error patterns with high accuracy. However, we always recommend human review for high-stakes situations. Think of us as your first line of defense — we catch the obvious stuff so you don't overpay.

How long does it take?

Quick Scan: 24 hours. Full Audit: 48 hours. Navigator: 72 hours. Rush processing available for urgent situations.
